# Logo Setup Instructions

Your website is ready! You just need to add your logo images.

## Required Logo Files

Please add the following logo files to the `/public` directory:

### 1. `dc-logo.png` - Small Logo (for Navigation)
- This should be your "DC" icon logo with the cursor arrow
- Recommended size: 60x60px or larger (will be displayed at 60x60px)
- Format: PNG with transparent background
- This is used in the header navigation

### 2. `dc-full-logo.png` - Full Logo (for About Section)
- This should be your full "DC Design Custom" logo
- Recommended size: 400x400px or larger
- Format: PNG with transparent background
- This is used in the About section

## How to Add Your Logos

1. Save your logo image files from your computer
2. Rename them to exactly:
   - `dc-logo.png` (small logo)
   - `dc-full-logo.png` (full logo)
3. Copy both files into the `/public` folder of this project
4. Refresh your browser - the logos will appear automatically!
